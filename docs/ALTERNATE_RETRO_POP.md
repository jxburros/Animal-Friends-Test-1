# Soft Retro Pop alternate art

Two 4 × 4 atlases add 32 Alternate Art printings in a deliberately different visual language:
mid-century editorial gouache, restrained pop-art geometry, warm paper, muted silkscreen color and
subtle halftone texture. The scenes interpret the cards' flavor and character stories rather than
copying their regular compositions.

- `assets/art/alternate-retro-after-hours-atlas.png`
- `assets/art/alternate-retro-quiet-triumphs-atlas.png`

The source atlases are preserved unchanged. `src/ui/versions.js` registers each scene with a
`#tile=0..15` fragment, and `src/ui/painted-art.js` crops that row-major tile into the art window.
