# Folk-Art Linocut alternate art

Two 4 × 4 atlases add 32 Alternate Art printings in a handmade folk-art linocut language: chunky
carved outlines, simplified silhouettes, deliberately uneven edges, rough natural-paper grain,
dense decorative patterns and slightly misregistered block-print color. The restricted black,
vermilion, mustard, teal and paper palette binds the two sheets together. Every tile interprets a
specific detail from the card's flavor rather than recreating its regular composition.

- `assets/art/alternate-linocut-working-paws-atlas.png`
- `assets/art/alternate-linocut-borough-rituals-atlas.png`

`src/ui/versions.js` registers each scene with a `#tile=0..15` fragment, and
`src/ui/painted-art.js` crops the row-major tile into the card's art window.
