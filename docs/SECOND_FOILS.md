# Second foil printings

The first fifteen foils were [drawn at random](FIRST_FOILS.md). These thirty-five were **chosen**:
ten of the cutest cards got artwork foil, ten of the coolest got reverse foil, ten of the most
underrated got full-card foil, and five cards whose paintings hold a detail worth shining got a
masked detail foil. Every card here had no Foil printing of its own before, and each uses its
existing ordinary artwork, so no new painting was commissioned. With the drawn fifteen and the
fourteen later hexagons that makes **64 ordinary Foil printings**.

The list, with the reason each card was picked, is recorded in
[FOIL_CHOICES.json](FOIL_CHOICES.json). Nothing here touches rules, rarity or deck eligibility:
a finish is presentation only.

## Five new detail masks

Each of these paintings has one piece of metal or glass in it that the sweep can pick out, and
nothing else in the scene competes with it. The masks are traced to the card's **ordinary** square
artwork, the one the Foil printing uses, and live in `assets/art/foil-masks/<card id>-foil.svg`.

| Card | Card ID | What shines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sage — Astronomer | `mk_sage_astronomer_3` | The brass telescope barrel and the pillars of its stand. |

brass until it gives a reflection back.

## The three older masks were retraced

The first release's three detail masks were traced to paintings that have since been replaced, and
two of them no longer landed on anything:

- **Finn — Auctioneer's Boy** shone a gavel and two candle flames that are not in the current
  painting; its shapes sat on the fox's coat and on the bunting. It now traces the two lantern
  flames and the brass candlesticks on the block.
- **Earl — Tea House Keeper** shone a teapot and cup rims well below and to the right of the real
  ones, over the middle of the table and the scones. It now traces the porcelain teapot and cups
  on the table and the brass lantern in front of it.
- **Comet — Astronaut** was close but loose: the helmet stroke crossed the glass, and the comet
  shape sat above its tail. All three details — helmet rim, chest gauge and comet — were retraced
  to the painting.

Every mask in `assets/art/foil-masks/` is now traced from the card's own atlas tile, so the shine
follows the metal that is actually painted there. If one of these paintings is replaced again,
retrace its mask; a mask that has drifted off its details is the one failure the renderer cannot
catch, because an inaccurate mask still animates perfectly well.

Open **The Book**, pick the **Foil** printing, and the whole run is there; the
[finish preview](../src/ui/foil-preview.html) can compare any card against the other finishes.
